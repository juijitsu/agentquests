# Theory · A whole shift

Nine levels took one failure at a time. Here there are four at once, and holding
them separately in your head will not work: each one breaks against its
neighbour.

## What happens over the shift

Three events wake the dispatcher. Handling the breakdown near Memphis spawns a
fourth — hire an outside carrier at triple price. It is irreversible, so it needs
a human. The human agrees. And at exactly that moment the process dies.

Four duties, all at once:

| What is needed | Where the skill came from | What it breaks against |
|---|---|---|
| Re-read the queue | level 07 | the irreversible is born **mid-shift** |
| Bother the human for good reason | level 09 | the queue grows, attention does not |
| Survive a crash | level 08 | it arrives **between the yes and the doing** |
| Keep the loop finite | level 02 | live reading takes finiteness away |

## The genuinely new thing here

On level 08 the state was "what I managed to do". That will not work here, and
the reason is worth understanding.

**The queue belongs to the world.** It does not live in your process, and a crash
does not threaten it: handled actions are already struck off, the remaining ones
wait for the next run. There is no need to record "what is done" — the world
remembers that itself.

**But the human's consent was known only to you.** It was spoken inside your
process and died with it. The world does not know about it, the queue does not
store it, the person considers the matter settled.

Hence a formulation sharper than level 08's:

> **State is what only you knew.**

Not everything, not "just in case", but precisely the part that exists nowhere
else. Recording what the world remembers anyway is extra work and an extra source
of disagreement.

## The cost of a second question

An agent that did not remember the consent comes back up and asks again.
Formally nothing terrible: the person answers yes, the action happens, the shift
closes.

In substance you spent a request to a human on a question you had already been
answered. On level 09 attention was a budget; here you can see the budget drains
not only from unnecessary questions but from **repeated** ones.

## The order inside a round

It is not arbitrary:

1. ask the queue — otherwise you are working with the past;
2. decide whether it is irreversible — otherwise you either bother the human for
   nothing or fail to bother them when it counts;
3. get approval, if it is not already approved — otherwise a second question;
4. execute — last, because up to that moment you can still change your mind.
