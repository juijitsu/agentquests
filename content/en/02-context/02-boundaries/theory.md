# Theory · A line with no source

On the previous level you learned not to dump the whole folder but to select
against the question. The technique works, and the same technique creates a new
failure.

**Selection cuts by lines, and documents are made of lines for a reason.**

## What is lost in selection

A carrier's rate sheet is a heading with the name and conditions beneath it. The
name appears once, and after it come lines that are nameless on their own:

```
Carrier Delta
rate 2.10 per mile
we do not haul reefer
minimum order 150 miles
```

Ask "what do we have about reefers" and you get three lines from three different
rate sheets, stacked into one list. The selection is flawless: nothing extra,
exactly on topic. And not one of those lines can now be traced to its owner.

## How the model goes wrong

It does not "make things up". It does exactly what a person reading an unlabelled
list does: **it attaches the fact to the nearest anchor.** No signature — take
the first line.

In our case the first one is Ridge's surcharge. The question was about Delta. The
answer comes out confident, specific, with a number — and belongs to somebody
else entirely.

That is more dangerous than an empty answer. An empty answer is visible at once;
this one looks like a working system right up to the day somebody checks it
against the rate sheet.

## The boundary is part of the data

Hence a rule worth remembering word for word:

> **A piece of text with no source attached is not data but hearsay.**

The document title, the author, the date, the version number — none of that is
decoration. It is part of the fact. Remove it while assembling the context and
the fact stops being checkable: neither the model nor a person can say where it
came from.

## Why not "pass whole documents"

The temptation is understandable: put the headings back by sending the rate
sheets in full. That fixes the attachment and breaks the previous level — twelve
lines instead of three, and what matters drifts back into the middle.

The right move is cheaper: **keep the selection and give every line its signature
back.** Three lines, three sources, nothing extra.
