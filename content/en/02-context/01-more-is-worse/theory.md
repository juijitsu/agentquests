# Theory · Fits does not mean found

The Agent Core track left you with a habit: watch that what matters does not fall
out of the window. Level 03 cut the history and lost the terms of the task, level
04 delivered the notepad into the window. Both taught the same thing — **get the
fact inside**.

Here the fact is inside. The answer is wrong anyway.

## The middle of a long context is not read

Twenty-two papers were sent to the model in full. The Carroll bridge limit —
eighteen tons against a twenty-four-ton load — sits thirteenth. It did not fall
out, was not truncated, was not lost: it is there.

And the model answers that the route is clear.

The cause is not volume as such but **where** inside a long text the needed thing
sits. The beginning and the end are read reliably, the middle is not. The longer
the context, the wider that dead zone.

On this level the rule is stated strictly and checkably: **the model reads six
blocks in full, and from a long list it sees only the first three and the last
three.** Real models have a softer boundary, but they behave the same way.

## A non-obvious consequence

Adding to the context "just in case" is neither free nor harmless. Every extra
paper does not merely take up room: it **pushes the needed one deeper**, where it
stops being read.

Twenty-two papers of which three matter work worse than three papers. Not because
the model got tired, but because you buried the answer in the middle with your
own hands.

## What to do instead

Select against the question rather than filing by mere existence. The difference
is in the phrasing:

- "what do we have on this haul" — a folder of twenty-two papers;
- "what bears on this question" — three lines about limits.

The second is the context engineer's job. Not to gather more, but **to decide
what not to include**.

## The border with the Retrieval track

Here the index is already given: the `about` tool returns papers by topic. How
that index is built — how to split documents, how to rank, what to do when
nothing fits — is a separate track. For now the task is simpler and more
important: **use the selection instead of ignoring it.**
